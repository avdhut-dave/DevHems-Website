DevHems — Contact Form 7 template: SERVICE ENQUIRY FORM
Used on: every Service detail page (single-service.php auto-fills the
"interested_service" hidden/select field with the current service title —
see inc/template-tags.php: devhems_cf7_service_shortcode())
=========================================================================
NOTE ON AUTO-SELECTION
Contact Form 7 tags cannot read PHP variables directly, so the theme
resolves this with a small wrapper shortcode: place
[devhems_service_enquiry_form] on the service template instead of the raw
[contact-form-7] shortcode. The wrapper (in inc/template-tags.php) renders
this form and injects a hidden field with the current post title as the
value for "interested_service", and pre-fills the visible dropdown to
match via forms.js.
