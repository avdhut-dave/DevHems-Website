DevHems — Contact Form 7 template: GENERAL CONTACT FORM
Used on: Contact Us page (full form)
========================================================
HOW TO USE
1. Plugins > Add New > install & activate "Contact Form 7" and "Flamingo".
2. Contact Form 7 > Add New, title it "General Contact Form".
3. Paste the FORM block below into the Form tab.
4. Paste the MAIL block into the Mail tab (this is the admin notification).
5. Paste the MAIL (2) block into the Mail tab's second tab (the visitor's
   acknowledgement email) — enable "Mail (2)" first.
6. Save, copy the shortcode, and drop it on the Contact Us page inside an
   Elementor "Shortcode" widget.
7. Under Contact Form 7 > Integration, connect Google reCAPTCHA (v3) or
   configure Cloudflare Turnstile per that plugin's own instructions —
   both integrate with CF7 without editing this template.
