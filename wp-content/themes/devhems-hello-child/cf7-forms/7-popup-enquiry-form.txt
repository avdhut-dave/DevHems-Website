DevHems — Contact Form 7 template: POPUP ENQUIRY FORM
Triggered by: every "Start Your Project" CTA button (header, hero, and
final-CTA sections). Built as an Elementor Pro Popup (Templates > Popups)
with an open-on-click trigger tied to the CTA buttons' ID/class — NOT an
auto-open-on-page-load popup, per the requirement to avoid intrusive
popups. If Elementor Pro is unavailable, use inc/mega-menu.php's pattern
to build a lightweight accessible dialog (role="dialog", aria-modal="true",
focus trap, Escape to close) around this same form markup.
